About DRYPOP

Small studio.

DRYPOP is a Seoul-based independent storytelling production company focused on ideas, narratives and visual worlds.

We develop stories from the first spark of an idea to a structure that can live across digital content, branded projects and original creative work.

We stay small by design, building each project carefully and shaping the process around the story.

DRY → POP

DRYPOP was built around a simple idea:

Take something ordinary and give it a little more color, imagination and joy.

We believe stories do not always need to be bigger.
Sometimes they simply need to make a familiar moment feel different.

Why Stories

Stories change the texture of things.

A story can turn information into curiosity.

It can give a product a personality, an idea a world, or an ordinary moment a reason to be remembered.

That is why we begin with story before format.

Before deciding what something should look like, we ask what it should make people feel, imagine or remember.

How We Work

Small by design.

DRYPOP keeps its core small and flexible.

Different stories need different combinations of people and skills, so the production approach can change with the project.

Instead of forcing every idea through the same process, we build the process around the story.

The DRYPOP Mascot

A little guide through big stories.

Our mascot moves from one story world to another — sometimes reading, sometimes exploring, sometimes getting into a little trouble.

It represents the curiosity at the center of DRYPOP: the instinct to look at an ordinary idea and wonder what else it could become.
